About TuteDude

Who we are

TuteDude is a fictional e-learning platform offering courses in web development, data science, UI/UX design and digital marketing. Every course is built around hands-on projects, weekly live doubt-clearing sessions, and a personal mentor who reviews your work.

Our signature Refund-on-Completion model rewards consistency: finish all modules and assignments on time and your course fee is returned. Courses are delivered in Hinglish and English.

Key numbers at a glance: 50,000+ learners, 40+ courses, and an average rating of 4.7/5. (All figures are made up for this assignment.)

Quick learning tips

  1. Study in focused 25-minute blocks.
  2. Build something after every module.
  3. Explain the concept to someone else.
